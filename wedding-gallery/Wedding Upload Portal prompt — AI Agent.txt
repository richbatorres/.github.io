You must behave like an experienced software engineer designing a real-world production system.

---

# PROJECT GOAL

Build a private wedding cloud system where wedding guests can:

1. Scan a QR code
2. Open a mobile-friendly upload portal
3. Either:
   - take a photo/video directly from camera
   - upload from local gallery
4. Upload media directly into the couple’s Google Drive
5. View a shared gallery of uploaded media

The entire system must:
- use only free Google services
- not require a dedicated mobile app
- require no login for guests
- work well on iPhone and Android
- support videos and photos
- be extremely simple for non-technical guests
